## 📊 Key Concepts

### Analysis Techniques

**Trend Analysis:**
- Track metrics over time
- Identify growth or decline
- Forecast future performance
- Example: Follower growth month-over-month

**Comparative Analysis:**
- Benchmark against competitors
- Compare content types
- Evaluate campaign performance
- Example: Your engagement vs. industry average

**Pattern Recognition:**
- Identify what works
- Spot anomalies
- Find correlations
- Example: Posts with images get 2x engagement

### Visualization Principles

**Chart Selection:**
- Line charts: Trends over time
- Bar charts: Comparisons
- Pie charts: Proportions (use sparingly!)
- Scatter plots: Correlations
- Heatmaps: Time/day patterns

**Design Best Practices:**
- ✅ Clear labels and titles
- ✅ Consistent color scheme
- ✅ Remove clutter (high data-ink ratio)
- ✅ Appropriate scale
- ✅ Context and benchmarks
- ❌ 3D charts (distorts data)
- ❌ Too many colors
- ❌ Misleading scales
