## 📊 ACTIVITY 1: Trend Analysis Workshop

**Time:** 20 minutes  
**When:** 10:40 - 11:00 AM  
**Format:** Individual work, then group discussion

### Objectives:
- Identify different trend types
- Calculate growth rates
- Draw insights from trends

### Materials Needed:
- [ ] Student workbook (trend analysis section)
- [ ] Calculators or Excel
- [ ] Projector for sharing results
- [ ] Timer

### Setup (5 min before):
1. Ensure all students have workbooks
2. Load example data on screen
3. Prepare to display results
4. Review answer key

---

### Part 1: Identify Trend Types (10 min)

**Instructions to Students:**

"You have 3 datasets showing different metrics over time. For each:
1. Plot the data (mentally or on paper)
2. Identify the trend type (Linear, Exponential, Seasonal, Declining)
3. Write one insight about what this means"

**Dataset 1: Monthly Followers**
```
Jan: 5,000
Feb: 5,100
Mar: 5,200
Apr: 5,300
May: 5,400
Jun: 5,500
```

**Dataset 2: Weekly Engagement Rate**
```
Week 1: 5.2%
Week 2: 5.8%
Week 3: 6.7%
Week 4: 8.1%
Week 5: 10.4%
Week 6: 13.8%
```

**Dataset 3: Quarterly Post Reach**
```
Q1: 50,000
Q2: 65,000
Q3: 45,000
Q4: 60,000
Q1: 48,000
Q2: 68,000
```

**Students work individually:** 8 minutes

**Instructor actions:**
- Set timer for 8 minutes
- Walk around to monitor progress
- Note students who are struggling
- Prepare to review

---

### Part 2: Review & Discussion (10 min)

**Call on students for answers:**

**Dataset 1 Answer:**
- **Trend Type:** Linear growth
- **Reasoning:** Consistent +100 followers per month
- **Insight:** Steady, predictable growth
- **Action:** Current strategy is working; continue

**Discussion points:**
- "Is linear good or bad?" (Depends on goals!)
- "Would you expect this to continue forever?" (No, eventually plateaus)

**Dataset 2 Answer:**
- **Trend Type:** Exponential growth
- **Reasoning:** Growth rate accelerating (not same amount each time)
- **Insight:** Something is working VERY well - possibly viral content
- **Action:** Identify what's causing acceleration, replicate

**Discussion points:**
- "What might cause exponential growth?" (Viral content, influencer mention)
- "Can this continue forever?" (No, eventually slows)

**Dataset 3 Answer:**
- **Trend Type:** Seasonal pattern
- **Reasoning:** Repeating high (Q2, Q4) and low (Q1, Q3) pattern
- **Insight:** Performance varies by quarter, likely tied to business cycle
- **Action:** Plan content and budget around seasonal patterns

**Discussion points:**
- "What might cause this pattern?" (Holiday shopping, summer lull)
- "How would you plan for this?" (Prepare for peaks, manage expectations in valleys)

---

### Success Indicators:
✅ Students correctly identify all 3 trend types
✅ Can explain reasoning
✅ Draw actionable insights
✅ Understand no single trend is "better"

### Common Struggles:
⚠️ Confusing linear with exponential
- **Solution:** Show visually - linear is straight, exponential curves up

⚠️ Not seeing seasonal pattern
- **Solution:** "Look for repeating highs and lows"

⚠️ Can't think of insights
- **Solution:** Ask "What would you do with this information?"

## 📊 ACTIVITY 2: Comparative Analysis Exercise

**Time:** 15 minutes  
**When:** 10:55 - 11:10 AM (extends slightly into next section)  
**Format:** Individual calculation, group review

### Objectives:
- Perform valid comparisons
- Calculate relative performance
- Identify best performer

### Materials Needed:
- [ ] Workbook with comparison data
- [ ] Calculators
- [ ] Answer key ready

### Setup:
Display data on screen so everyone can see clearly

---

### The Exercise:

**Instructions to Students:**

"You're comparing 3 coffee shop Instagram accounts. Calculate engagement rate and engagement per 1,000 followers to determine who's performing best."

**Data Provided:**

| Account | Followers | Avg. Likes | Avg. Comments | Avg. Shares | Avg. Post Reach |
|---------|-----------|------------|---------------|-------------|-----------------|
| Coffee A | 25,000 | 800 | 40 | 15 | 12,000 |
| Coffee B | 15,000 | 750 | 60 | 25 | 10,000 |
| Coffee C | 35,000 | 900 | 30 | 10 | 18,000 |

**Tasks:**
1. Calculate engagement rate for each account
2. Calculate engagement per 1,000 followers
3. Rank the accounts (1st, 2nd, 3rd place)
4. Write one sentence explaining the winner

**Formula Reminders:**
- Engagement Rate = (Total Engagements / Reach) × 100
- Total Engagements = Likes + Comments + Shares
- Per 1K = (Engagement / Followers) × 1,000

**Time:** 8 minutes to calculate

---

### Work Time (8 min):

**Instructor actions:**
- Set timer
- Walk around to help
- Check calculations
- Note common errors
- Don't give answers yet!

**Common issues to watch for:**
- Forgetting to add all engagement types
- Using followers instead of reach for engagement rate
- Calculation errors

---

### Review (7 min):

**Coffee A:**
```
Total Engagements: 800 + 40 + 15 = 855
Engagement Rate: (855 / 12,000) × 100 = 7.125%
Per 1K Followers: (855 / 25,000) × 1,000 = 34.2
```

**Coffee B:**
```
Total Engagements: 750 + 60 + 25 = 835
Engagement Rate: (835 / 10,000) × 100 = 8.35%
Per 1K Followers: (835 / 15,000) × 1,000 = 55.67
```

**Coffee C:**
```
Total Engagements: 900 + 30 + 10 = 940
Engagement Rate: (940 / 18,000) × 100 = 5.22%
Per 1K Followers: (940 / 35,000) × 1,000 = 26.86
```

**Rankings:**

**By Engagement Rate:**
1. Coffee B - 8.35%
2. Coffee A - 7.125%
3. Coffee C - 5.22%

**By Engagement Per 1K:**
1. Coffee B - 55.67
2. Coffee A - 34.2
3. Coffee C - 26.86

**Winner: Coffee B!**

**Key Insight:** "Coffee B has the smallest following but the highest engagement rate and per-capita engagement. They're building a more engaged community."

---

### Discussion Points:

**Q:** "Why does Coffee C have lower rates despite most likes?"
**A:** Larger reach means engagement is diluted. Raw numbers don't tell whole story.

**Q:** "Which metric matters more - engagement rate or per 1K followers?"
**A:** Depends on goal. Engagement rate shows content quality. Per 1K shows community strength.

**Q:** "What advice would you give each account?"

- **Coffee A:** Good performance, maintain course
- **Coffee B:** Excellent engagement, focus on growth while maintaining quality
- **Coffee C:** Need to improve content relevance to boost engagement

---

### Success Indicators:
✅ Accurate calculations
✅ Correct ranking
✅ Understand relative performance
✅ Can explain why B wins despite smaller following

### Extension (if time):
"Calculate weighted engagement (Likes×1, Comments×3, Shares×5). Does ranking change?"

- **Intensity:** Strongly positive

- **Answer:** ❌ **Negative**
- **Reasoning:** "Not worth" is criticism, disappointed expectation

**Comment 17:** "Smells amazing and works great"

- **Reasoning:** Two positive attributes
- **Intensity:** Positive

**Comment 18:** "I prefer your old formula to be honest"

- **Reasoning:** Question, demographic inquiry
- **Note:** Opportunity to expand market appeal

**Comment 20:** "Absolutely love this brand! Never disappointed"
- **Answer:** ✅ **Positive**
- **Reasoning:** Brand loyalty + consistent satisfaction
- **Intensity:** Very strongly positive

---

### Sentiment Score Calculation:

**Count:**
- ✅ Positive: 8 comments (#1, 2, 7, 10, 12, 15, 17, 20)
- ↔️ Neutral: 6 comments (#3, 5, 9, 11, 14, 19)
- ❌ Negative: 6 comments (#4, 6, 8, 13, 16, 18)

**Formula:**
```
Sentiment Score = (Positive - Negative) / Total × 100
Sentiment Score = (8 - 6) / 20 × 100
Sentiment Score = 2 / 20 × 100
Sentiment Score = +10
```

**Interpretation:** +10 = **Slightly Positive / Neutral-Positive Mix**

---

### Discussion Questions:

**Q:** "Is +10 good or bad?"
**A:** It's neutral-to-slightly-positive. Room for improvement. Equal negative and positive (minus neutrals) means issues to address.

**Q:** "Which negative comments are most urgent?"
**A:** #4 (product reaction - health concern) and #8 (order delay - service failure). Both need immediate response.

**Q:** "How could you improve the score?"
**A:**
- Address product reactions (reformulation?)
- Fix fulfillment delays
- Address price concerns
- Respond to those preferring old formula

**Q:** "What would a -50 score mean?"
**A:** Crisis level - major problems, mostly negative comments. Immediate action required.

**Q:** "What about +80?"
**A:** Excellent! Overwhelmingly positive. Keep doing what you're doing.

---

### Discussion: Edge Case - Comment #11

**"Meh, it's okay I guess"**

This one often creates disagreement!

**Arguments for Neutral:**
- Technically not negative words
- "Okay" could mean acceptable

**Arguments for Negative:**
- "Meh" expresses disappointment
- "I guess" shows doubt
- Lack of enthusiasm = weak negative

**Teaching Point:**
"Sentiment analysis is somewhat subjective. In teams, you'd calibrate together. Many brands would count this as 'weak negative' because it won't drive repurchase."

---

### Success Indicators:
✅ 80%+ agreement on clear positives/negatives

- **Solution:** "Look for frustration, even if politely expressed"

## 📊 ACTIVITY 4: Chart Selection Challenge

**Time:** 15 minutes  
**When:** 2:00 - 2:15 PM  
**Format:** Pairs, competitive

### Objectives:
- Match scenarios to appropriate chart types
- Justify chart selection
- Critique wrong choices

### Materials Needed:
- [ ] 10 scenario cards
- [ ] Chart type reference sheet
- [ ] Whiteboard for answers
- [ ] Small prizes (optional)

### Setup:
- Pair students
- Provide scenario list
- Set competitive tone (fun!)

---

### The Challenge:

**Instructions to Students:**

"In pairs, you have 10 scenarios. Choose the BEST chart type for each. Write your answers. First pair done correctly wins!"

**10 Scenarios:**

**Scenario 1:** Show how your follower count changed every month for a year
- **Options:** Line, Bar, Pie, Scatter, Heatmap
- **Best Answer:** LINE CHART
- **Why:** Time series data, continuous trend

**Scenario 2:** Compare average engagement rate across Instagram, Facebook, Twitter, and TikTok
- **Options:** Line, Bar, Pie, Scatter, Heatmap
- **Best Answer:** BAR CHART
- **Why:** Comparing discrete categories

**Scenario 3:** Display age breakdown of your audience (18-24: 40%, 25-34: 35%, 35-44: 15%, 45+: 10%)
- **Options:** Line, Bar, Pie, Scatter, Heatmap
- **Best Answer:** PIE CHART or BAR CHART
- **Why:** Parts of whole, limited categories

**Scenario 4:** Investigate if posting more frequently leads to higher engagement
- **Options:** Line, Bar, Pie, Scatter, Heatmap
- **Best Answer:** SCATTER PLOT
- **Why:** Correlation between two variables

**Scenario 5:** Find the best time to post by analyzing engagement across days of week and hours of day
- **Options:** Line, Bar, Pie, Scatter, Heatmap
- **Best Answer:** HEATMAP
- **Why:** Two dimensions (day × hour), pattern finding

**Scenario 6:** Show how total revenue from social campaigns has grown each quarter for 2 years
- **Options:** Line, Bar, Pie, Scatter, Heatmap
- **Best Answer:** LINE CHART or AREA CHART
- **Why:** Time series, showing growth trend

**Scenario 7:** Compare performance of 5 recent posts (which got most likes)
- **Options:** Line, Bar, Pie, Scatter, Heatmap
- **Best Answer:** BAR CHART
- **Why:** Comparing discrete items (5 posts)

**Scenario 8:** Display traffic sources to your website (35% from Instagram, 25% from Facebook, 20% from Google, 15% from Twitter, 5% other)
- **Options:** Line, Bar, Pie, Scatter, Heatmap
- **Best Answer:** PIE CHART or BAR CHART
- **Why:** Percentages that add to 100%, limited categories

**Scenario 9:** Track daily active users on your platform over the past 90 days
- **Options:** Line, Bar, Pie, Scatter, Heatmap
- **Best Answer:** LINE CHART
- **Why:** Daily time series, looking for trends

**Scenario 10:** See if accounts with more followers also get more likes per post
- **Options:** Line, Bar, Pie, Scatter, Heatmap
- **Best Answer:** SCATTER PLOT
- **Why:** Relationship between two continuous variables

---

### Competition (10 min):

**Rules:**
1. Work in pairs
2. First to finish raises hand
3. Instructor checks answers
4. If all correct, they win!
5. If mistakes, they revise and others catch up
6. Keep going until we have a winner

**Instructor actions:**
- Monitor time
- Check answers quickly
- Provide hints if everyone is stuck
- Keep energy high!

---

### Review (5 min):

**Go through tricky ones:**

**Most debated: Scenario 3 (Pie vs. Bar)**
- Both are acceptable!
- Pie shows proportions well with 4 categories
- Bar is easier to compare exact percentages
- "Choose based on what you want to emphasize"

**Most debated: Scenario 6 (Line vs. Area)**
- Line is simpler, shows trend clearly
- Area shows cumulative growth, visual impact
- Area good if showing stacked categories
- "Either works! Line is more common."

**Most debated: Scenario 8 (Pie vs. Bar)**
- Same as #3
- Pie emphasizes whole/parts relationship
- Bar easier to read exact values
- "When in doubt, use bar chart!"

---

### Key Teaching Points:

**Pie Chart Skepticism:**
"Notice we could use bar charts for both pie chart scenarios? That's because bar charts are more versatile. Pie charts are fine for 3-5 categories, but bar charts work for everything."

**Scatter Plot Confusion:**
"If you're looking for a relationship/correlation, think scatter plot. Are two things related? Scatter plot."

**Heatmap for Patterns:**
"Heatmaps are perfect for time-based patterns with two dimensions. Best posting time? Heatmap."

---

### Success Indicators:
✅ At least 8/10 correct
✅ Can justify selections
✅ Understand multiple answers can work
✅ Know when pie chart is acceptable (and when it's not)

### Winner Prize Ideas (optional):
- First choice of seat for team challenge
- Extra credit point
- Social media analytics sticker
- "Chart Master" certificate (printed quickly)

"Let's create our first metric card. Watch me, then you do it."

```

3. Select B2 (label) → Bold

6. Fill color: Light blue (or brand color)

**Check:** "Everyone have Total Followers showing a number? Raise hand if yes!"

**Metric 2: Engagement Rate**

```

*This pulls from pre-calculated average engagement rate.*

**Format:**
- Same styling as Metric 1
- Fill color: Light green

**Tip:** "Make sure you see a percentage, like 6.8%"

---

**Metric 3: Total Reach**

```
Cell H2: "Total Reach"
Cell H3: =SUM(Data!E:E)
```

**Format:**
- Same styling
- Fill color: Light orange

---

**Metric 4: Posts This Month**

*Subtract 1 to exclude header row.*

**Format:**
- Same styling

Show thumbs up if you have all four! 👍"

5. Center align

**Add Metadata:**

Cell A3: "Prepared by: [Your name]"

**Clean Up:**

1. View tab → Uncheck "Gridlines"
   (Makes background clean white)

2. Align all elements:
   - Metric cards at top
   - Charts in middle
   - Even spacing

3. Consistent colors:
   - Make sure colors complement each other
   - Not too bright or jarring

4. Final review:
   - Can you read everything clearly?
   - Does it look professional?
   - Any misaligned elements?

### Troubleshooting Guide:

**Issue:** Formula returns #REF! error
- **Cause:** Incorrect cell reference
- **Fix:** Check formula refers to correct tab and column

**Issue:** Chart shows weird data
- **Cause:** Wrong cells selected
- **Fix:** Delete chart, reselect correct range, insert again

**Issue:** Formatting doesn't apply
- **Cause:** Wrong cells selected
- **Fix:** Click correct cells, reapply formatting

**Issue:** "My dashboard looks messy"
- **Cause:** Elements not aligned
- **Fix:** Use View → Gridlines to help align, then turn off

**Issue:** Can't see formulas working
- **Cause:** Calculation set to manual
- **Fix:** Formulas → Calculation Options → Automatic

### The Scenario:

```
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                  THE FITNESS BRAND CHALLENGE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

CLIENT: FitLife Gym (Instagram account for gym chain)

SITUATION:
Your manager needs a dashboard for their monthly board meeting
showing social media performance and recommendations.

REQUIREMENTS:
1. Overall performance summary
2. Best content types (what's working?)
3. Optimal posting times (when should we post?)
4. One clear recommendation for next month

DELIVERABLE:
Dashboard + 2-minute presentation

TIME: 20 minutes to build, 2 minutes to present

DATA PROVIDED:
- 30 days of post data
- Metrics: engagement, reach, content type, posting time
- Follower growth data
```

---

### Team Formation (2 min):

**Assign roles (teams self-select):**
- **Data Analyst:** Focuses on calculations, data accuracy
- **Designer:** Focuses on layout, colors, visual appeal
- **Insight Generator:** Identifies patterns, writes recommendations
- **Presenter:** Prepares and delivers 2-min presentation

"Everyone contributes, but having roles keeps you organized!"

---

### Work Time (20 min):

**Start timer!**

**Instructor actions:**
- Set visible countdown timer
- Circulate to all teams
- Don't help too much (it's a challenge!)
- Provide hints only if team is completely stuck
- Give time warnings: 10 min left, 5 min left, 1 min left

**What to observe:**
- Team collaboration
- Role distribution
- Creative solutions
- Time management
- Problem-solving

**When timer hits zero:**
"Hands off! Time's up! Save your dashboards."

---

### Presentation Round (2 min per team × 4-5 teams = 10 min):

**Presentation Structure (enforce strictly):**

1. **Show Dashboard** (30 sec)
   - Screen share/project dashboard
   - Quick tour of elements

2. **Key Insight** (30 sec)
   - "What did you discover?"
   - Most important finding

3. **Recommendation** (30 sec)
   - "What should FitLife do next month?"
   - Specific, actionable advice

4. **Questions** (30 sec)
   - Instructor asks one question
   - Team answers

**Keep strict time:** Have a timer visible. Gently cut off if over.

---

### Sample Team Presentations:

**Team 1 Example:**

"Our dashboard shows [point to screen]:
- Engagement rate: 7.2% (up from 6.5%)
- Reels massively outperform (11% vs. 5% for photos)
- Best posting time: 6-8 PM weekdays

KEY INSIGHT:
Evening workout posts get 3x engagement because that's when our audience is at the gym or thinking about fitness.

RECOMMENDATION:
Schedule 70% of posts for 6-8 PM window, prioritize Reels over photos, focus on evening workout content.

[Q&A]"

### Judging Criteria:

**Score each team on:**

1. **Dashboard Quality (30 points)**
   - Correct calculations (10)
   - Clear visualizations (10)
   - Professional design (10)

2. **Insights (30 points)**
   - Insight is clear (10)
   - Backed by data (10)
   - Business-relevant (10)

3. **Recommendation (20 points)**
   - Specific and actionable (10)
   - Logical from data (10)

4. **Presentation (15 points)**
   - Clear communication (8)
   - Timing (stayed under 2 min) (7)

5. **Teamwork (5 points)**
   - Collaboration observed (5)

**Total: 100 points**
