### SLIDE 16: Solution

```
ENGAGEMENT PER 1,000 FOLLOWERS

You:
  720 / 12,000 × 1,000 = 60 per 1K

Competitor A:
  1,000 / 25,000 × 1,000 = 40 per 1K

Competitor B:
  960 / 8,000 × 1,000 = 120 per 1K

🏆 Winner: Competitor B

💡 Key Insight: Follower count ≠ Engagement!
```

### SLIDE 24: Weighted Engagement

```
CALCULATING WEIGHTED ENGAGEMENT

Standard Formula:
  Likes + Comments + Shares = Total

Weighted Formula:
  (Likes × 1) + (Comments × 3) + (Shares × 5)

Example:
  100 likes + 20 comments + 5 shares

Standard:  125 engagements
Weighted:  (100×1) + (20×3) + (5×5) = 185 value

+48% more value when weighted!
```

### SLIDE 34: Sentiment Score Calculation

```
SENTIMENT SCORE FORMULA

Sentiment Score = (Positive - Negative) / Total × 100

From our example:
Positive: 3
Neutral: 3
Negative: 4
Total: 10

Score = (3 - 4) / 10 × 100 = -10

INTERPRETATION:
+50 to +100: Very positive
+10 to +50:  Positive
-10 to +10:  Neutral/Mixed
-50 to -10:  Negative
-100 to -50: Very negative
```

**Presenter Notes:**
- Walk through calculation
- -10 = slightly negative overall
- "This brand needs to address issues"
