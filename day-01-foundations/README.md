## 🎓 Key Concepts

### Essential Metrics to Know

**Awareness Metrics:**
- **Reach:** Number of unique users who saw your content
- **Impressions:** Total number of times content was displayed
- **Follower Growth:** Net new followers over time

**Engagement Metrics:**
- **Engagement Rate:** (Likes + Comments + Shares) / Reach × 100
- **Amplification Rate:** Shares / Followers × 100
- **Applause Rate:** Likes / Followers × 100
- **Virality Rate:** Shares / Impressions × 100

**Conversion Metrics:**
- **Click-Through Rate (CTR):** Clicks / Impressions × 100
- **Conversion Rate:** Conversions / Clicks × 100
- **Cost Per Click (CPC):** Total Spend / Total Clicks
- **Return on Ad Spend (ROAS):** Revenue / Ad Spend

**Customer Metrics:**
- **Response Time:** Average time to respond to messages
- **Response Rate:** % of messages responded to
- **Customer Satisfaction Score:** Survey ratings

## 📝 Activities and Exercises

### Morning Activities

**Activity 1: Metrics Brainstorm (15 min)**
- Small groups list all social media metrics they know
- Share and categorize on board
- Instructor fills gaps

**Activity 2: Vanity vs. Actionable (20 min)**
- Given list of metrics, students categorize
- Discuss which metrics actually drive business decisions
- Examples: 10K followers vs. 5% conversion rate

**Activity 3: Platform Tour (30 min)**
- Live demonstration of each platform's analytics
- Students follow along on their own accounts
- Q&A throughout

### Afternoon Activities

**Activity 4: Data Collection Simulation (45 min)**
- Students access their own social media analytics
- Record key metrics in provided template
- Identify trends or patterns
- Share findings with partner

**Activity 5: Ethics Case Study (30 min)**
- Present real-world privacy violation case
- Group discussion: What went wrong?
- How could it have been prevented?
- What are the consequences?

**Activity 6: Metric Scavenger Hunt (45 min)**
- Teams of 3-4 students
- Find specific metrics on different platforms
- First team to complete worksheet wins
- Debrief: What was easy? What was hard?
