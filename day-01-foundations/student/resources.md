## Platform Analytics Access Guides

### Facebook Business Suite

**Access URL:** https://business.facebook.com

**How to Access:**
1. Go to business.facebook.com
2. Log in with your Facebook account
3. Select your Page from the dropdown
4. Click "Insights" in the left sidebar

**Requirements:**
- Facebook Page (not personal profile)
- Admin or Editor role
- At least 30 followers for full insights

**What You'll Find:**
- Overview Dashboard: Key metrics at a glance
- Content: Post performance analysis
- Audience: Demographics and behavior
- Inbox: Message analytics (if applicable)

**Pro Tips:**
- Change date ranges to compare periods
- Export data for deeper analysis
- Check "When Your Fans Are Online" for optimal posting times
- Use "Top Posts" to identify successful content patterns

### Instagram Insights

**Access Methods:**

**Mobile App (Recommended):**
1. Go to your profile
2. Tap the three horizontal lines (menu)
3. Tap "Insights"

**Desktop (via Business Suite):**
1. Go to business.facebook.com
2. Select your Instagram account
3. Click "Insights"

**Requirements:**

**What You'll Find:**

**Minimum Requirements:**
- 100+ followers for full insights
- Recent activity (posts in last 30 days)

## Metrics Glossary

### Audience Metrics

**Followers/Fans**
- Definition: Number of people who subscribe to your account
- Platforms: All
- Why it matters: Audience size, but vanity without engagement
- Formula: Current total count

**Follower Growth Rate**
- Definition: Percentage increase in followers over time
- Formula: (Net New Followers / Starting Followers) × 100
- Benchmark: 2-5% monthly is healthy
- Why it matters: Shows momentum and attraction

**Audience Demographics**
- Definition: Characteristics of your followers (age, gender, location, interests)
- Platforms: All (varying detail)
- Why it matters: Ensures you're reaching target audience
- How to use: Tailor content to audience profile

---

### Reach & Impression Metrics

**Reach**
- Definition: Number of unique people who saw your content
- Platforms: All
- Why it matters: Measures actual audience exposure
- Note: Always ≤ Impressions

**Impressions**
- Definition: Total number of times content was displayed
- Platforms: All
- Why it matters: Shows total exposure (including multiple views)
- Formula: Sum of all views

**Organic Reach**
- Definition: People who saw content without paid promotion
- Platforms: Facebook, Instagram
- Why it matters: Shows natural content spread
- Note: Declining on most platforms (algorithm changes)

**Paid Reach**
- Definition: People who saw content through advertising
- Platforms: All (with ads)
- Why it matters: Measures ad effectiveness
- Trackable: Via ad manager tools

---

### Engagement Metrics

**Likes/Reactions**
- Definition: Quick approval/acknowledgment of content
- Value: Low (easy, low commitment)
- Platforms: All
- Why it matters: Shows content resonance

**Comments**
- Definition: Text responses to content
- Value: Medium-High (requires effort)
- Platforms: All
- Why it matters: Indicates deeper engagement, conversation

**Shares/Retweets**
- Definition: User redistributes your content to their audience
- Value: High (extends reach, strong endorsement)
- Platforms: All
- Why it matters: Amplifies message, signals strong agreement

**Saves (Instagram)**
- Definition: User bookmarks content for later
- Value: Very High (strong intent signal)
- Platform: Instagram
- Why it matters: Heavily weighted by algorithm, indicates valuable content

**Engagement Rate**
- Definition: Percentage of audience that interacted
- Formula: (Total Engagements / Reach) × 100
- Benchmark: 1-5% average, 5%+ excellent
- Why it matters: Normalizes engagement for comparison

---

### Conversion Metrics

**Click-Through Rate (CTR)**
- Definition: Percentage who clicked a link
- Formula: (Clicks / Impressions) × 100
- Benchmarks:
  - Facebook: 0.90%
  - Instagram: 0.22%
  - Twitter: 1-3%
  - LinkedIn: 0.26%
- Why it matters: Measures call-to-action effectiveness

**Conversion Rate**
- Definition: Percentage who completed desired action
- Formula: (Conversions / Total Clicks) × 100
- Benchmark: 2-3% for e-commerce
- Why it matters: Directly measures business outcomes

**Cost Per Click (CPC)**
- Definition: Average cost for each link click
- Formula: Total Ad Spend / Total Clicks
- Platform-specific: Varies widely
- Why it matters: Measures advertising efficiency

**Cost Per Acquisition (CPA)**
- Definition: Average cost to acquire customer/lead
- Formula: Total Ad Spend / Total Conversions
- Why it matters: Measures true ROI of campaigns

**Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)**
- Definition: Revenue generated per dollar spent
- Formula: Revenue / Ad Spend
- Benchmark: 4:1 is good (4× return)
- Why it matters: Proves profitability

---

### Video Metrics

**Video Views**
- Definition: Number of times video was watched
- Platforms: All
- Note: Different platforms define "view" differently
  - Facebook: 3 seconds
  - Instagram: 3 seconds
  - YouTube: 30 seconds
  - TikTok: Based on watch time

**Average Watch Time**
- Definition: How long people watch on average
- Formula: Total Watch Time / Total Views
- Why it matters: Indicates content quality
- Goal: Higher is better, aim for 50%+ completion

**Completion Rate**
- Definition: Percentage who watched entire video
- Formula: (Complete Views / Total Views) × 100
- Benchmark: 30%+ is good
- Why it matters: Shows compelling content

**Video Engagement Rate**
- Definition: Interactions relative to views
- Formula: (Likes + Comments + Shares) / Views × 100
- Why it matters: Measures video resonance

---

### Story Metrics (Instagram/Facebook)

**Story Views**
- Definition: Number of times story was viewed
- Note: Multiple views by same person count separately

**Story Reach**
- Definition: Unique accounts that viewed story
- Always ≤ Story Views

**Story Exits**
- Definition: Times someone left your story before completion
- Why it matters: High exits = content not compelling

**Story Replies**
- Definition: Direct messages sent in response
- Why it matters: High engagement signal

**Completion Rate**
- Definition: Percentage who watched entire story sequence
- Formula: (Final Frame Views / First Frame Views) × 100
- Benchmark: 70%+ is good
- Why it matters: Measures story quality

## Formula Reference Sheet

### Essential Calculations

**Engagement Rate**
```
Engagement Rate = (Total Engagements / Reach) × 100

Where:
Total Engagements = Likes + Comments + Shares [+ Saves for Instagram]
```

**Click-Through Rate (CTR)**
```
CTR = (Clicks / Impressions) × 100
```

**Conversion Rate**
```
Conversion Rate = (Conversions / Clicks) × 100
```

**Follower Growth Rate**
```
Growth Rate = (Net New Followers / Starting Followers) × 100

Where:
Net New Followers = New Followers - Unfollowers
```

**Average Engagement Per Post**
```
Avg Engagement = Total Engagements Across All Posts / Number of Posts
```

**Post Frequency**
```
Posts Per Day = Total Posts / Number of Days
```

**Reach Rate**
```
Reach Rate = (Reach / Followers) × 100
```

**Amplification Rate**
```
Amplification = (Shares / Reach) × 100
```

**Virality Rate**
```
Virality = (Shares / Impressions) × 100
```

---

### Advanced Calculations

**Cost Per Click (CPC)**
```
CPC = Total Ad Spend / Total Clicks
```

**Cost Per Thousand Impressions (CPM)**
```
CPM = (Total Ad Spend / Impressions) × 1,000
```

**Cost Per Acquisition (CPA)**
```
CPA = Total Ad Spend / Total Conversions
```

**Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)**
```
ROAS = Revenue from Ads / Total Ad Spend

Or as ratio:
ROAS = Revenue / Spend (e.g., 4:1 means $4 earned per $1 spent)
```

**Return on Investment (ROI)**
```
ROI = [(Revenue - Cost) / Cost] × 100
```

**Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)**
```
CLV = Average Purchase Value × Average Purchase Frequency × Customer Lifespan
```

**Weighted Engagement Score**
```
Weighted Score = (Likes × 1) + (Comments × 3) + (Shares × 4) + (Saves × 5)
```

## Privacy Laws and Ethics

### GDPR Overview

**Full Name:** General Data Protection Regulation

**Applies to:** EU citizens (regardless of where business is located)

**Key Requirements:**
1. **Consent:** Must obtain clear consent before collecting data
2. **Right to Access:** Users can request their data
3. **Right to be Forgotten:** Users can request data deletion
4. **Data Portability:** Users can transfer data to another service
5. **Breach Notification:** Must notify within 72 hours
6. **Privacy by Design:** Build privacy into systems

**Penalties:** Up to €20 million or 4% of global revenue (whichever is higher)

**Social Media Impact:**
- Can't scrape user data
- Must have privacy policy
- Need consent for cookies/tracking
- Users can delete accounts and data

**Resources:**
- Official site: https://gdpr.eu
- Guide: https://gdpr.eu/what-is-gdpr/

---

### CCPA Overview

**Full Name:** California Consumer Privacy Act

**Applies to:** California residents

**Key Rights:**
1. Know what data is collected
2. Know if data is sold/shared
3. Opt-out of data sales
4. Delete personal data
5. Non-discrimination for exercising rights

**Requirements for Businesses:**
- Privacy policy on website
- "Do Not Sell My Info" link
- Respond to requests within 45 days

**Penalties:** $2,500 per violation ($7,500 if intentional)

**Resources:**
- Official site: https://oag.ca.gov/privacy/ccpa

---

### COPPA Overview

**Full Name:** Children's Online Privacy Protection Act

**Applies to:** Children under 13 in the US

**Key Requirements:**
1. Parental consent for data collection
2. Clear privacy policy
3. Limited data collection
4. Data security measures
5. Parental access to child's data

**Social Media Platforms:**
- Minimum age: 13 for most platforms
- Some platforms 16+ (e.g., WhatsApp)
- TikTok restrictions for under-16

**What You Can't Do:**
- Target children under 13
- Collect data from children without parental consent
- Use behavioral advertising to children

---

### Ethical Guidelines Checklist

**Before collecting data, ask:**
- [ ] Do I have permission/consent?
- [ ] Am I following platform Terms of Service?
- [ ] Am I complying with GDPR/CCPA/COPPA?
- [ ] Am I being transparent about data use?
- [ ] Am I collecting only necessary data?
- [ ] Is data stored securely?
- [ ] Can users access/delete their data?
- [ ] Am I respecting user privacy?
- [ ] Would I be comfortable if this were my data?
- [ ] Is this in users' best interest?

## Troubleshooting Guide

### Common Issues and Solutions

**Issue:** Can't find Instagram Insights

**Solutions:**
- ✅ Switch to Professional Account (Settings → Account → Switch Account Type)
- ✅ Need 100+ followers for full insights
- ✅ Wait 24-48 hours after switching to professional
- ✅ Ensure account is public

---

**Issue:** Facebook Business Suite not showing data

**Solutions:**
- ✅ Check you have admin/editor role on the page
- ✅ Page needs 30+ likes for some insights
- ✅ Try refreshing browser or clearing cache
- ✅ Check selected date range

---

**Issue:** Engagement rate seems wrong

**Solutions:**
- ✅ Verify formula: (Engagements / Reach) × 100
- ✅ Don't use impressions - use reach
- ✅ Include all engagement types (likes + comments + shares + saves)
- ✅ Check if using the right time period

---

**Issue:** Can't export data

**Solutions:**
- ✅ Check browser allows downloads
- ✅ Try different browser
- ✅ Some mobile apps don't support export - use desktop
- ✅ Check file format compatibility

---

**Issue:** Numbers don't match across tools

**Solutions:**
- ✅ Different tools calculate differently
- ✅ Check date ranges match exactly
- ✅ Some tools use estimates
- ✅ Time zones might differ
- ✅ Real-time data vs. cached data

---

**Issue:** Low engagement rates

**Solutions:**
- ✅ Check posting times - align with audience activity
- ✅ Analyze top posts for patterns
- ✅ Try different content types
- ✅ Engage with audience (respond to comments)
- ✅ Use relevant hashtags
- ✅ Post consistently
