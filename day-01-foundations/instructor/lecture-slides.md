## Slide 19: Reach vs. Impressions

```
📊 REACH VS. IMPRESSIONS

REACH
= The number of UNIQUE people who saw your content
= How many different individuals

IMPRESSIONS  
= The TOTAL number of times your content was displayed
= Counts multiple views by same person

EXAMPLE:
• 1,000 people saw your post (REACH)
• It was viewed 3,500 times total (IMPRESSIONS)
• Some people saw it 2, 3, or more times!
```

**Presenter Notes:**
- Use storefront analogy
- "Reach = people who walked by. Impressions = times they walked by"
- **Timing:** 0:50-0:53

## Slide 22: Calculating Engagement Rate

```
📈 ENGAGEMENT RATE FORMULA

Engagement Rate = (Total Engagements / Reach) × 100

WHERE:
Total Engagements = Likes + Comments + Shares

EXAMPLE:
• Post received 50 likes
• 10 comments  
• 5 shares
• Reached 1,000 people

Engagement Rate = (50 + 10 + 5) / 1,000 × 100
                = 65 / 1,000 × 100
                = 6.5%

Industry Average: 1-5% is typical
6%+ is excellent!
```

**Presenter Notes:**

## Slide 24: Click-Through Rate (CTR)

```
🖱️ CLICK-THROUGH RATE (CTR)

CTR = (Number of Clicks / Impressions) × 100

Measures: How many people clicked your link

EXAMPLE:
• Post with link got 10,000 impressions
• 250 people clicked the link

CTR = 250 / 10,000 × 100 = 2.5%

BENCHMARKS:
• Facebook: 0.90% average
• Instagram: 0.22% average  
• Twitter: 1-3% average
• LinkedIn: 0.26% average

Higher CTR = More compelling content
```

**Presenter Notes:**
- Critical for driving traffic
- "This is what gets people OFF platform"
- **Timing:** 1:04-1:07

## Slide 25: Growth Metrics

```
📈 GROWTH METRICS

FOLLOWER COUNT
• Total number of followers
• Vanity metric unless tracked over time

FOLLOWER GROWTH RATE
= (New Followers / Total Followers) × 100

EXAMPLE:
• Started month with 1,000 followers
• Gained 150 new followers
• Lost 20 followers
• Net gain: +130

Growth Rate = 130 / 1,000 × 100 = 13% growth

Healthy growth: 2-5% monthly
```

**Presenter Notes:**
- Distinguish between total and rate
- "Direction and speed both matter"
- **Timing:** 1:07-1:10

## Slide 26: Conversion Metrics

```
💰 CONVERSION METRICS

CONVERSION = When someone takes desired action

Examples:
• Sign up for newsletter
• Download app
• Make purchase
• Fill out form
• Register for event

CONVERSION RATE = (Conversions / Total Clicks) × 100

EXAMPLE:
• 500 people clicked link to product page
• 25 people made a purchase

Conversion Rate = 25 / 500 × 100 = 5%

E-commerce average: 2-3%
```

**Presenter Notes:**
- Connect to business goals
- "This is where social drives revenue"
- **Timing:** 1:10-1:13

ACTIONABLE METRICS

✅ Lead to concrete decisions

   • Engagement rate
   • Conversion rate
   • Click-through rate
   • Cost per acquisition

Focus on ACTIONABLE metrics!
