#### 11:15 - 12:00 PM: Key Metrics and KPIs (45 min)

**Objectives:**
- Define essential metrics
- Calculate engagement rates
- Distinguish vanity from actionable metrics

**11:15 - 11:30: Core Metrics** (15 min)

Present each metric with visual:

**Reach vs. Impressions:**
```
Reach = 1,000 unique people saw your post
Impressions = 3,500 total views (some people saw it multiple times)
```

Use analogy: "Reach is how many people walked past your storefront. Impressions is how many times total they walked past."

**Engagement Metrics:**
- Likes, Comments, Shares, Saves
- Why each matters differently
- Platform-specific: Retweets, Repins, etc.

**Engagement Rate Formula:**
```
Engagement Rate = (Likes + Comments + Shares) / Reach × 100
```

**Example Calculation:**
```
Post has: 50 likes, 10 comments, 5 shares
Reach: 1,000 people
Engagement Rate = (50 + 10 + 5) / 1,000 × 100 = 6.5%
```

**Growth Metrics:**
- Follower count
- Follower growth rate
- Audience retention

**11:30 - 11:45: Vanity vs. Actionable** (15 min)

**Vanity Metrics:**
- Look impressive but don't drive business
- Examples: Total followers, total likes
- Why they're misleading

**Actionable Metrics:**
- Directly tied to business goals
- Examples: Conversion rate, CTR, ROI
- Lead to concrete decisions

**Activity: Categorize the Metrics** (10 min)
- Show 15 different metrics on board
- Students vote: Vanity or Actionable?
- Discuss surprising answers
- Key insight: "It depends on your goals!"

Metrics list:
1. 10,000 followers (Vanity - unless growing audience IS the goal)
2. 5% conversion rate (Actionable)
3. 100,000 impressions (Could be either)
4. 8% click-through rate (Actionable)
5. [Continue...]

#### 2:45 - 3:30 PM: Ethics and Privacy (45 min)

**Objectives:**
- Understand data privacy laws
- Recognize ethical dilemmas
- Learn best practices

**2:45 - 3:00: Legal Framework** (15 min)

**Key Laws:**

**GDPR (EU):**
- User consent required
- Right to access data
- Right to be forgotten
- Applies to EU citizens anywhere

**CCPA (California):**
- Similar to GDPR
- Privacy rights for California residents
- Affects many US companies

**Children's Privacy:**
- COPPA protects under-13
- Special rules for children's data
- Platform age restrictions

**2:00 - 3:15: Ethical Considerations** (15 min)

**Principles:**

1. **Transparency**
   - Tell users you're collecting data
   - Explain how it will be used
   - Privacy policy must be clear

2. **Consent**
   - Don't scrape data without permission
   - Respect platform ToS
   - Opt-in > Opt-out

3. **Data Security**
   - Protect personal information
   - Secure storage
   - Limited access

4. **Responsible Use**
   - Don't manipulate or mislead
   - Consider impact on individuals
   - Think beyond profit

**Common Violations:**
- Scraping user data illegally
- Selling personal information
- Tracking without consent
- Manipulating vulnerable users

**3:15 - 3:30: Case Study Discussion** (15 min)

**Case: Cambridge Analytica Scandal**

Present scenario:
- Facebook data harvested
- 87 million users affected
- Used for political targeting
- No informed consent

**Discussion Questions:**
1. "What went wrong?"
2. "Who was responsible?"
3. "What were the consequences?"
4. "How could it have been prevented?"
5. "What lessons for us as future marketers?"

**Break into groups of 3-4:**
- 5 minutes to discuss
- Each group shares one insight
- Instructor synthesizes lessons

**Key Takeaways:**
- Ethics isn't optional
- Violations have serious consequences
- Your responsibility as a professional
- "Just because you can, doesn't mean you should"

**Materials:**
- Privacy law summary sheet
- Ethics guidelines handout
- Case study details
- Discussion prompts

**Teaching Tips:**
- Don't be preachy - facilitate discussion
- Use recent, relevant examples
- Connect to career implications
- Acknowledge grey areas exist

**Objectives:**
- Practice accessing analytics
- Record real data
- Identify patterns

**Activity: Metric Scavenger Hunt**

**Setup:** (5 min)
- Divide into teams of 3-4
- Each team gets worksheet
- First team to complete correctly wins small prize

**The Hunt:** (30 min)

Teams must find and record:

**On Facebook:**
1. Total page likes for [Brand X]
2. Reach of most recent post
3. Best performing post this month
4. Audience gender breakdown
5. Peak activity time

**On Instagram:**
1. Follower count for [Influencer Y]
2. Average likes on recent 5 posts
3. Story view rate
4. Profile visits this week
5. Top location of audience

**On Twitter:**
1. Tweet impressions for [Company Z]
2. Engagement rate of recent tweet
3. Follower growth this month
4. Link click-through rate
5. Top mention or hashtag

**Rules:**
- Use only free/available tools
- No guessing - must find actual data
- Screenshot or document source
- Work together as team

**Debrief:** (10 min)
- Which metrics were easiest to find?
- Which were hardest?
- What surprised you?
- What did you learn about platforms?

**Materials:**
- Scavenger hunt worksheets
- List of accounts to analyze

**Teaching Tips:**
- Choose accessible accounts
- Have answers ready to verify
